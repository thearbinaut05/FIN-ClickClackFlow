Organization checklist

  1. Preserve an authorized backup/export before changing anything.
  2. Match each real payment to its provider account, mode, currency, units and transaction ID.
  3. Separate approved receivables, internal credits, simulations and unmatched records.
  4. Review overly broad write policies and ledger-changing routine permissions separately.
  5. Enable no payout until funds, ownership and payout eligibility are verified.

This dashboard does not change records, run legacy monetization routines, or reconcile individual entries automatically. Other Stripe accounts, banks and affiliate networks require their own evidence.
